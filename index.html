    Rest between sets
    Warm-ups: 45–60s. Working (failure) sets: 2–3 min. The timer auto-starts when you tick a set — warm-ups 60s, working sets 150s. If the next row is a finisher (F), it drops to a 20s rest-pause gap instead. Adjust with ±15s or skip.
    Finisher sets
    For rest-pause or drop sets: log your straight set at its true failure reps (e.g. 10), then tap + Finisher to add an F row for the extra reps (e.g. 6) at the same weight. Keeps your working-set number clean for tracking while still counting the extra work. Tap any set number to flip it between working and F.
    Rest-pause
    Hit failure, rack it, breathe 15–20s, squeeze out 2–4 more at the same weight. Log those extra reps as a finisher (F) set.
    Negatives
    Lower the weight over 8–10s. Past failure, keep doing slow lowers for 2–3 more.
    Static hold
    At the hardest point, hold the contraction 10–20s after your last full rep.
    Pre-exhaust
    Isolation move to failure, then straight into the compound with no rest. (Second machine taken? Rest-pause the single machine, or use the bodyweight finisher.)
    Technical failure
    Barbell squat & RDL only: stop when form slips — never grind a loaded bar past failure while training solo. No rest-pause or forced reps on these.
